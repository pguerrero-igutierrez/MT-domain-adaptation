1 Motivation & Research Questions

Catalan–Basque (CA–EU) MT is severely under-resourced, especially in specialized domains. Standard multilingual models produce poor output for this pair, and in-domain parallel data is scarce.

Core question: Can synthetic domain-specific corpora built from related bilingual resources substantially improve CA–EU MT over a general baseline?
1
Can pivot-based corpus construction and back-translation create useful training data for low-resource domains?
2
Does domain-specific fine-tuning improve in-domain translation quality over the general model?
3
Is continued fine-tuning from a general checkpoint better than direct domain-specific fine-tuning from the base model?
2 Two Domains, Two Quality Criteria
Literary
Quality depends on fluency, creativity and stylistic naturalness.
Clinical
Quality depends on terminological accuracy and clinical clarity.

10 Key Findings
1
generalv1 strongly improves general CA-EU MT: chrF++ 13.69 → 43.49 and COMET 46.85 → 80.68 overall, with near-perfect length ratios (~0.986).
2
Cross-domain transfer helps but is insufficient: generalv1 applied out-of-domain beats the zero-shot baseline but falls clearly below dedicated domain models on both literary and clinical tasks.
3
Direct domain SFT outperforms continued adaptation: literaryv1 > literaryv2 and clinicalv1 > clinicalv2 on all reported metrics — continued adaptation from a general checkpoint is not uniformly better.
4
clinicalv1 achieves the largest relative gain: chrF++ 11.03 → 40.79 (+29.8 pp), confirming that synthetic data via round-trip back-translation is effective even with only 66 test items.
5
Pipeline inconsistencies identified & corrected: v2 scripts use Spanish↔Basque (ES-EU) auxiliary data — not CA-EU as labelled. clinicalv2 uses eval_loss + epoch-eval (not BLEU+steps). eu2ca literary uses text_ca, not text_ca_back. Two separate models handle translation (Latxa-Llama-3.1) vs fine-tuning (Latxa-Qwen3).
11 Limitations & Future Work
Limitations: Small clinical test set (N=66); single-directional clinical training; reliance on synthetic supervision; incomplete requirements.txt; some corpus schemas unresolved.
Corpus schema
Unify file naming; document exact provenance and field definitions of each synthetic corpus.
Ablations
Test pivot language choices, backtranslation strategy and domain oversampling weights.
Curriculum
Compare direct SFT, continued fine-tuning and mixed-domain curricula under a controlled protocol.
Bidirectional
Extend clinical to eu→ca; add human evaluation and larger test sets.
